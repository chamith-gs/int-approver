Add AMS File

Upload an Excel (.xlsx / .xls) or CSV file with exactly 4 columns: Part No, then three monthly invoiced-quantity columns named <Month>_Invoiced Quantity. The months can change every upload. The average of the three months becomes the AMS. The upload is saved to the server and replaces the previous AMS table.
